Accessibility Statement

How we work to keep thenolangroup.com usable for every visitor, and how to reach us if something on this site gets in your way.

The Nolan Group wants every visitor to be able to find a home, reach our team, and read our content on thenolangroup.com, regardless of how they browse the web.

What we aim for

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. This is an ongoing effort rather than a finished state, and parts of the site may not yet fully conform.

What we have done so far

Text is rendered as real text rather than images. Page zoom and browser text resizing are not blocked. Navigation works with a keyboard, and meaningful images carry alternative text. Heading levels follow a single order on each page.

Known limitations

Some property photography and third-party embedded content is supplied by outside providers, and we do not always control its markup. Where an embed cannot be made accessible, contact us and we will supply the same information another way.

Tell us about a barrier

If any part of this site blocks you, we want to hear about it and we will work with you directly. Email info@thenolangroup.com or call (772) 222-3327. Please tell us the page and what happened, and we will respond and offer the information in an accessible format.

Fair housing

The Nolan Group supports the Fair Housing Act and Equal Opportunity Act. We do business in accordance with federal, state, and local fair housing laws.
